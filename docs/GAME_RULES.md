# Rules

## Maps

Each map is divided into stages containing key points you need to clear by card battles. Maps vary in difficulty, depending on key points, length, and environment conditions. Three maps are available each day.

### Winning a map

A map is finished once you complete the final stage, abandon, or restart it. Completion of a map grants all rewards earned, plus a completion bonus. Defeating all cards applies a perfect completion bonus. Abandoning the map keeps all rewards earned so far without the bonus. Restarting removes all rewards, cards and modifiers earned and resets all cards, modifiers spent during the run.

## Stages

Each map is divided into sequential stages. You can only clear key points at the current stage. Once you advance to the next stage, you cannot return to previous ones. Each stage brings you closer to the final goal of the map.

### Stage goals

In each stage, you need to complete a goal by defeating a certain number of enemy cards. You don't have to defeat every enemy card, but doing so can earn additional reward and improve your chances in a later stages. In the final stage, you will encounter the primary target, a powerful enemy card, you must defeat it along with other enemies to complete the map.

## Key points

Each stage contains several key points, each with its own enemy deck, rewards, environment conditions. While you do not have to clear every key point in a stage, you can choose key points based on risk, rewards, and future strategic impact. Key points vary in difficulty, on how strong the enemies are.

### Your deck

Each card can be deployed to only one key point per stage. Every time a card participates in a battle, it loses 1 hp and carries the remaining hp into all subsequent stages of the map. sending your strongest cards into a single key point may improve the chances of victory but spreading your deck across multiple key points can give you greater flexibility and access to extra rewards.

## Cards

### Total power

Total power is the primary value used in battle and calculated as the sum of three card parameters (firepower, survivability, manueverability) plus or minus 10 points variation. Total power is also affected by buffs from your other cards and current hp.

### Card HP

Each card has three HP. Every time a card participates in a battle, it loses 1 HP. Lower HP reduces a card's parameters and as a result its total power:

3 hp -> 100% parameters
2 hp -> 90% parameters
1 hp -> 80% parameters
0 hp -> cannot be used

All cards restore full hp at the start of each new map. Hp is fully restored upon restarting a map.

## Enemy Card

### Enemy power

Each enemy has a fixed power value. In battle, the higher your total power range relative to the enemy's power, the better the chances of victory. If enemy power exceeds your total power range, victory is impossible. If it falls below, victory chance is 100%. Increase your odss by using buffs or exploiting enemy vulnerabilities with debuffs.

### Vulnerabilities

Each enemy can have up to two vulnerabilities:

1. AA Fire,
2. Smoke Screen,
3. Detection,
4. Large Caliber,
5. Quick Fire,
6. Air Strike.

Different enemies have different vulnerabilities. So you can pick cards maximizing most effective debuffs.

## Buffs and Debuffs

### Buffs

Buffs increase one parameter of an adjacent card on your playfield. Each buff applies in a specific direction, left or right. And only affects the card in that position. A card can have up to two buffs in total. Arrange your deck carefully to maximize buff efficiency before battle and check enemy cards before committing your cards.

### Debuffs

Debuffs reduce an enemy's power, but only if the debuff type matches one of the enemy's vulnerabilities. Each debuff targets a specific position. Diagonal left, straight ahead, diagonal right. Debuffs that do not match a vulnerability have no effect.

## Environment

### Battle Conditions

Some key points have battle conditions, special effects that modify battles. They can strengthen enemies, weaken your cards, restrict cards that can be deployed, or disable modifiers. Local battle conditions always apply to the key point that generates them, they cannot be removed. Targeted and global battle conditions are generated by earlier key points.

### Targeted and Global Conditions

Targeted battle conditions affect specific key points in the next stage. Global battle conditions affect every key point in all future stages in the map. Defeat the enemies in a key points to weaken or remove the conditions they generate. Choosing which key points to clear early can make later ones significantly easier.

## Modifiers

Modifiers are optional items that can be applied before a battle. They can increase parameters, restore HP, improve rewards or help counter difficult battle conditions. There are two types of modifiers. Standards (grey) and Special (green). Standard ones can be obtained with supplies and earned battle rewards, or received from map rewards. Special modifiers can only be obtained from defeating specific enemy cards.

### Modifier slots

Each card has two modifier slots, one standard, one special. A modifier affects only the card it is applied to for that key point. Modifiers gained or spent during a map are restored to their starting state if you restart the map. You can have N number of each modifiers in your inventory. If you use a modifier, that quantity is reduced by 1.

### Standard Grey Modifiers

Can be bought using the points 

1. Gunnary: Firepower +20% (Cost: 12 pt, Qty: n1)
2. Damage Control: Survivability +20% (Cost: 12 pt, Qty: n2)
3. Dodge: Maneuverability +20% (Cost: 12 pt, Qty: n3)
4. Teamwork: Buff power x2 (Cost: 12 pt, Qty: n4)
5. Effectiveness: Reward tokens +10% (Cost: 18 pt, Qty: n5)
6. Morale: If the affected enemy is defeated, it counts as 2 towards current stage goal and towwards removing battle conditions (Cost: 12 pt, Qty: n6)

### Special Green Modifiers

1. Focused: Disable all buffs and debuffs, increases all parameters of your card +40% (Qty: s1)
2. Specialization: Debuff power x2 (Qty: s2)
3. Rabid: Debuff can be applied even if the enemy does not have matching vulnerability (Qty: s3)
4. Fast Repair: Restores HP Segments +2 (Qty: s4)
5. Unstoppable: Ignores battle conditions in the key point that afects your card's parameters buffs and debuffs (Qty: s5)
